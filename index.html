Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Details regarding security, amenities, car capacity, and vehicle entry.

What is the capacity of Shoreline Private Parking at Kayalpattinam Beach?

Our facility accommodates 60+ cars simultaneously with both shaded roof bays and open spacious stalls. It also accommodates tourist tempo travellers, vans, and bikes.

Are clean restrooms and toilets available on premises?

Yes! We have clean, hygienic Pay & Use Toilets and fresh water wash areas specifically designed for tourists and families visiting Kayalpattinam Beach.

Can I charge my Electric Vehicle (EV) here?

Yes, we have installed dedicated EV Charging Stations on-site. You can plug in your EV or electric two-wheeler and relax on the beach while your vehicle charges.

How does the Car Wash Service work?

Beach winds often coat vehicles in salty air and sand. Our on-site wash team can thoroughly rinse, wash, and detail your car with clean fresh water while you are at the beach.

How do I reach the team for instant booking or questions?

You can directly call our enquiry hotlines at 99945 48247 or 97897 81673, or click our WhatsApp button to chat with our staff in seconds.
